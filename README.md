<h2>Children's Informant Judgments Across Information Contexts</h2>

<strong>Overview</strong>

This repository contains the study materials and analysis scripts for a developmental study examining children's judgments of the relative helpfulness of digital voice assistants (DVAs) and human informants across different information contexts.

The study examines children's third-party judgments of informant helpfulness in two contexts:

- Social-Personal Information: Information involving social relationships, emotional experiences, and interpersonal situations.

- Epistemic Information: Information involving factual knowledge, categories, and learning how to perform skills.

<strong>Repository Contents</strong>

01_Study_Materials/: Experimental procedures, stimuli, task instructions, and response materials.

02_Data_Analysis/: Statistical analysis scripts and supporting code.

<strong>Reproducibility</strong>

Materials and analysis scripts are shared to support transparency and reproducibility. Please consult the individual folders for descriptions of their contents.
